STARHOLD

Settle the frontier. Court the aliens. Outbuild your rivals.

How to play

Goal: first to 15 victory points during your own turn. Colonies 1 VP, starports 2, alien friendship medallions 2, captured hazard chips 1, every 2 renown stars 1 (max 5 VP from renown).

Turn: 1) Production: roll 2 dice; every planet with that number pays 1 card to each adjacent colony or starport. Under 9 VP you also draw 1 reserve card. 2) Trade and build, in any order, as often as you like. 3) Flight: roll your flagship, then move ships.

Flagship roll: two of four orbs show. Sun = 3, ember/comet = 2 each. A flare means a deep-space event and base speed 3. Total speed = base + thrusters (+ favors). All your ships share it.

Ships: you own exactly 3 transporters. Settler ships (1 Alloy, 1 Carbon, 1 Fuel, 1 Biomass) found colonies on the circled sites between two planets. Envoy ships (1 Alloy, 1 Fuel, 2 Goods) dock at alien stations: lowest open number first, and your cargo pods must be at least that number. New ships launch beside your starports. Founding returns the transporter for reuse.

Exploration: frontier planets hide their numbers. Fly adjacent to peek privately; found a colony to flip them face up. Raider dens fall to railguns, frozen worlds to cargo pods; each captured chip is 1 VP and the planet gets a fresh number.

Civilizations: four alien civilizations live somewhere out there, each spanning three hexes — but they start hidden. Fly a ship into their space to make first contact and reveal their stations. Each outpost earns a diplomatic favor; whoever holds the MOST outposts at a civilization holds its medallion (ties: lowest-numbered outpost).

Trading: bank 3:1, Goods always 2:1, favors improve rates. You may also trade 1:1 with other captains if they agree.

On a 7: no production; more than 7 cards means you discard half; the roller takes a random card from one rival.
