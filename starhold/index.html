STARHOLD

Settle the frontier. Court the aliens. Outbuild your rivals.

How to play

Goal: first to 15 victory points. Colonies are worth 1, starports 2, alien friendship medallions 2, cleared hazards 1, and every 2 renown stars 1.

Each turn: roll for production (planets matching the dice pay every adjacent colony), then trade and build, then roll your flagship. A flare on the flagship roll triggers a deep-space event. Then move your ships.

Ships: build settler ships to found colonies on open planet docks, and envoy ships to dock at alien stations (you need cargo pods equal to the station number). First envoy at each alien race earns a friendship medallion and a diplomatic favor.

Flagship: upgrade with thrusters (speed), railguns (fight off raiders), and cargo pods (alien trade). Renown stars come from events and bold deeds.

Hazards: raider dens need railguns >= their number; frozen worlds need cargo pods >= their number. Clearing one earns 1 VP and opens the planet.

Rolling a 7: no production; everyone holding more than 7 cards discards half, and you commandeer a random card from a rival.

Resupply: while you have fewer than 9 VP, you draw 1 free resource each turn.
